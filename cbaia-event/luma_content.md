## 活动标题 (Event Title)

**中文:**探索全球机遇：中英美投资人与华人创业者的炉边对话

**English:**Global Opportunities: A Fireside Chat with Investors from China, UK, and US for Global Chinese Entrepreneurs

---

## 活动描述 (Event Description)

**中文:**

由中英人工智能协会 (CBAIA) 主办，我们荣幸地邀请您参加一场独特的线下与线上混合式炉边谈话。本次活动将汇聚来自中国、英国和美国顶尖投资机构的资深投资人，与全球华人创业者社区进行深度对话。

我们深知许多海外华人创业者在面对全球资本市场时，常常感到困惑：应该去哪里融资？不同市场的投资人偏好和游戏规则是怎样的？本次活动正是为了解决这些痛点而生。

无论您是正在寻求种子轮融资的早期创业者，还是计划拓展国际市场的成熟企业家，这都将是一次宝贵的机会。您将了解到：

- **不同市场的投融资洞见**: 中、英、美三国风险投资生态的最新趋势、异同点以及各自的投资偏好。

- **投资人视角**: 顶级投资人如何评估初创公司？他们尤其关注海外华人创业者的哪些特质和潜力？

- **给创业者的建议**: 在当前的全球宏观经济环境下，创业者应如何制定融资策略、搭建团队并实现跨境增长？

活动将包括一个45分钟的圆桌讨论，一个30分钟的互动问答环节，以及一个长达1小时的线下社交网络环节（仅限伦敦现场参与者）。我们期待与您一同探索全球创业的新机遇！

**English:**

Hosted by the China-Britain Artificial Intelligence Association (CBAIA), you are cordially invited to a unique hybrid (in-person and virtual) fireside chat. This event brings together seasoned investors from leading firms in China, the UK, and the US for an in-depth conversation with the global Chinese entrepreneurial community.

We understand that many overseas Chinese entrepreneurs face a critical challenge: navigating the global capital landscape. Where should you fundraise? What are the distinct characteristics and investor preferences in different markets? This event is designed to address these very questions.

Whether you are an early-stage founder seeking seed funding or an established entrepreneur planning international expansion, this is an invaluable opportunity to gain insights into:

- **Cross-Market Investment Landscape**: Discover the latest trends, differences, and investment appetites of the venture capital ecosystems in China, the UK, and the US.

- **The Investor's Perspective**: How do top-tier investors evaluate startups? What specific qualities and potential do they look for in overseas Chinese entrepreneurs?

- **Actionable Advice for Founders**: In the current macroeconomic climate, how should you strategize your fundraising efforts, build your team, and achieve cross-border growth?

The event will feature a 45-minute panel discussion, a 30-minute interactive Q&A session, and a 1-hour in-person networking session in London. Join us to explore new frontiers in global entrepreneurship!

## 主办方 (About the Host)

**中文:**中英人工智能协会 (China-Britain Artificial Intelligence Association, CBAIA) 是一家在英国注册的非营利组织，致力于为中英两国的人工智能领域的专业人士、研究人员和创业者搭建一个分享行业知识、链接学术资源、促进商业合作的平台与社区。自2017年成立以来，CBAIA已汇聚超过600名会员，并通过举办高水平的论坛、研讨会和项目路演，积极推动着两国在AI创新与合作上的发展。

**English:**The China-Britain Artificial Intelligence Association (CBAIA) is a UK-registered non-profit organization dedicated to building a platform and community for AI professionals, researchers, and entrepreneurs in both China and the UK. Our mission is to facilitate the sharing of industry knowledge, connect academic resources, and promote business collaboration. Since its establishment in 2017, CBAIA has grown to over 600 members and actively fosters AI innovation and partnership between the two countries through high-level forums, seminars, and project showcases.
