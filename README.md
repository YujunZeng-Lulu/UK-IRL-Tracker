## 🌟 Features

The UK IRL Tracker is designed to be intuitive, private, and powerful. All data is stored locally in your browser, ensuring complete privacy and data ownership.

- **Absence Tracking**: Automatically calculates the number of days you've been away from the UK within any rolling 12-month period.
- **Interactive Calendar**: Visualize your entry, departure, and residency days on a clean, interactive calendar.
- **One-Click Marking**: Simply click a date on the calendar to mark it as a departure or arrival day, similar to tracking cycles in a health app.
- **Batch Operations**: Easily add, modify, or delete multiple date ranges at once for quick setup and correction.
- **Smart Alerts**: Receive timely warnings as you approach the 180-day absence limit.
- **Optimal Travel Suggestions**: Get intelligent recommendations for your next departure date to maximize your time abroad without risking your residency status.
- **ILR Countdown**: A built-in countdown timer shows you exactly how long until you're eligible to apply for Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR).
- **Privacy First**: No login required. All your data stays on your device, stored securely in your browser's local storage.

## 🚀 How to Use

Getting started is incredibly simple:

1.  **Initial Setup**: Enter your visa type and your first date of entry into the UK. The calendar will instantly visualize your timeline.
2.  **Mark Absences**: Click on any date in the calendar to toggle it as a departure day. Click again to unmark it.
3.  **Batch Input**: Use the batch input feature to add or modify multiple departure periods quickly.

Bookmark the site for easy access and check your status anytime!
